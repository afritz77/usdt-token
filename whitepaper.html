USDT Whale (USDTW) — Whitepaper

Version 1.0 · October 2026

Abstract

USDT Whale (USDTW) is a fixed-supply settlement token deployed simultaneously on Solana and Tron. 80,000,000 USDTW were minted once on each network — a hard total of 160,000,000 tokens — and no more can ever be created. Mint and freeze authorities are permanently revoked on both chains, verifiable on-chain by any party.

1. Overview

USDTW is designed as a settlement and payment token for cross-chain transfers and peer-to-peer value exchange, operating on two of the lowest-cost networks available:

  • Solana — sub-second finality, fractions of a cent per transfer
  • Tron — near-zero fees for TRC-20 transfers, wide exchange and wallet support

The same name, symbol, and supply law apply on both chains.

3. Fixed Supply & Security

  • All tokens were minted in a single issuance at launch (2026-10-07).
  • The mint authority is revoked: no additional USDTW can ever be created on either network.
  • The freeze authority is revoked: token holders cannot be frozen or confiscated by the issuer. Holders have unconditional control of their balances.
  • Both revocations are verifiable on-chain via the explorers listed in Section 6.

4. Markets & Liquidity

USDTW trades on decentralized exchanges on both networks:

  • Tron — SunSwap v2 pair (USDTW/WTRX), live and settled by market activity near a $1.00 reference price.
  • Solana — Raydium CPMM pool (USDTW/SOL), providing SOL-side liquidity for the network.

Current market data is available on DEX screeners and on the project website.
